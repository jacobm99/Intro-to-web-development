Our Mission

At Twin Cities Animal Rescue, we believe every animal deserves a second chance at a vibrant, loving life. Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome vulnerable pets while empowering our community through education and compassionate action. Together, we can build a future where every cage is empty and every home is full of love.

Our Impact This Year

  • 750+ animals successfully placed in loving forever homes.
  • 1,200 hours of dedicated volunteer community service.
  • 400+ pets nurtured in our trusted foster network.
  • 0 healthy or treatable animals euthanized.

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Open your heart and home to a pet in need.

  • Volunteer: Give your time at our shelter.
  • Donate: Fund medical care, food, and safe housing for our rescues.
