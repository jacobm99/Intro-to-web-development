About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2010 by a small group of passionate local advocates, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a local neighborhood workign together. We saw a critical gap in resources for stray and abandoned animals in our community and decided to take action. Today, we have grown into a trusted nonprofit facility, but our core values remains the same.

Success Spotlight

Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat all animals with empathy and respect.
  • Integrity: We operate with complete transparency to honor the trust of our donors and community.
  • Persistence: We do not give up on animals with medical or behavioral challenges.
  • Collaboration: We believe we are stronger together and actively partner with local businesses and shelters.

Partner Acknowledgments

Our life-saving work is made possible through the generous support of our community partners:

  • Twin Cities Veterinary Clinic - Providing discounted emergency medical care.
  • Happy Paws Pet Supply - Donating premium food and toys monthly.
  • The Daily Bark Foundation - Sponsoring our annual adoption events.
