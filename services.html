Our Services & Programs

Adoption Process

Finding your perfect companion is a journey we take together. Our adoption process is thorough but supportive to ensure lifelong matches:

  1. Application: Fill out a brief questionnaire about your lifestyle and household.
  2. Meet & Greet: Spend one-on-one time with potential pets in our playrooms.
  3. Counseling: Speak with our adoption specialists to prepare for bringing your new pet home.
  4. Welcome Home: Sign the adoption agreement and take your new best friend home!

Foster Program

Fostering saves lives. By taking an animal into your home temporarily, you open up space in our shelter for another urgent rescue. We provide all the medical care, food, and supplies. You provide the love, patience, and a comfortable couch. Fosters are especially needed for senior pets, nursing mothers, and animals recovering from surgery.

Volunteer Roles

Our volunteers are the backbone of Twin Cities Animal Rescue. Current opportunities include:

  • Dog Walkers: Ensure our canine residents get daily exercise, training reinforcement, and affection.
  • Cat Cuddlers: Help socialize shy or fearful cats so they can blossom into confident pets.
  • Event Ambassadors: Represent the rescue at community farmers' markets and adoption drives.
  • Shelter Assistants: Help with the essential daily tasks of cleaning, feeding, and organizing donations.
